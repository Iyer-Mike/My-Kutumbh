OneGetting in

One set of rules decides where a person lands, kept in a single place so nobody falls between two of them. An invitation always takes precedence over wherever they were headed.

Is an invitation waiting?
Yes → join that KutumbhThey become a member of an existing family
No → set up their ownName the Kutumbh, add height, weight, diet, Prakriti
The fault this fixed. The confirmation email used to lose the invitation, so an invited member was asked to start a family of their own — and then couldn't join, because nobody may leave a Kutumbh. Now the invite waits on their phone, and a person alone in a Kutumbh of their own can close it to accept an invitation. A family with other people in it is never left this way.

FourFrom meals and reports to advice

Insights are worked out fresh each time from what is already stored. Nothing is pre-computed, so a meal logged a minute ago counts immediately.

What goes inProfile · 30 days of meals · the latest lab report · the family's dishes
Four readings, each on its own Needs — ICMR-NIN targets for age, sex, weight and activity
Intake — what was actually eaten, per day and averaged
Ayurveda — tastes, heating or cooling, effect on the person's Prakriti
Labs — each reading against its normal range, grouped by concern
Cross-checks that connect them Medicines against food · allergies against what was eaten · conditions against the pattern · several lab values read together, such as high sugar with high triglycerides
What the family sees Health alerts & tips, energy, lab guidance, vitamins and minerals, Ayurveda, trends — and a coach that answers from these same numbers
What the app will not do. It never diagnoses, and never suggests starting, stopping or changing a medicine — those questions are sent to a doctor. Food guidance avoids anything a person is allergic to, and respects their diet strictly.
